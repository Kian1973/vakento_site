What is Vakento?

Vakento is business software for self-employed professionals and small businesses with customers, quotes, planning, time tracking, receipts, cloud, AI and a client portal.

Does Vakento work on a phone?

Yes. Vakento works in the browser and is designed for both phone and laptop use.

Can I scan receipts?

Yes. You can photograph a receipt and keep the original together with recognised data for your administration.
